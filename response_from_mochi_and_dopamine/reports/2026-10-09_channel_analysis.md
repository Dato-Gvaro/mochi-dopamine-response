## Diagnosis
- **The swipe in the first ~2 s is the bottleneck.** Stayed-to-watch averages 40.5%. Industry rule of thumb: about 50% is average, 70%+ gets pushed wider, below 30% dies (sources in research/hooks.md). The parent channel: 65%+ stay gave 5x median views.
- **Content holds once a viewer stays:** the low-stay clips have the highest average-viewed % (ring_escape2 140% = loops). The middle and end are fine; the opening loses people.
- **Frames 0-2 s, best three vs worst three:**
  - The best three are bright pastel, with big characters as the focus and a goal readable in one glance.
  - The worst three are dark, with small characters inside a busy machine; the goal reads only after a few seconds.
  - n = 9 and confounded (day, slot), so this is a HYPOTHESIS, but the new dark brain-satisfying batch with small characters is exactly the bottom-three look. The Oct 12+ uploads will test it.
- **Geography:** the test audience is PH 10%, GE 5%, ID 5%, BR 4%, US 2.6%. GE is most likely our own and friends' views.

## Owner decision
Keep the dark colour scheme as a deliberate test (2026-10-09). Judge it on the Oct 15-16 data. The hook rules (bright subject on frame 0) apply either way.
