## What the winners share
1. **Peril for the characters:** lava below, floors dropping, "who falls first" (collapse_floors, seesaw_lava). Danger plus elimination keeps people to the end.
2. **A simple, instantly readable race/elimination** with team identity (Team Mochi vs Team Bolt).
3. **A visible rule in the title** ("Every tile they touch DROPS", "Every rock pushes Bolt closer to the LAVA"), the reference channel's title style.
4. The brain-satisfying maths/physics type performs about the same as the rest (~1.1-1.5K), except beam_clash2 (a power-formula duel with energy beams), which got a second day.

## Recommendations
1. **Keep 3/day (or more):** volume is the clearest lever so far.
2. **Make a sequel of collapse_floors now,** with one nameable change (e.g. "Every floor they touch DROPS — now with 4 players" / "the floor is falling AND the lava rises"). Also grand_prix 2 (a new track with one twist) and seesaw_lava 3.
3. **Blend the winners with the new rules:**
   - characters in physical peril (lava, falling, collapse) + a choice duel + a visible rule in the title + a mid-action opening + material sounds;
   - the batch31/32 duels (demolition, siege, freefall) already fit; prefer stakes where Mochi/Bolt themselves are at risk.
4. **Titles:** lead with the rule ("Every X does Y") or the peril ("closer to the LAVA"); these out-performed clever titles.
5. **Next export:** in Studio Advanced mode add the columns "Stayed to watch (%)", "Engaged views", "Average percentage viewed". Without them we can't tell which openings hook.

Reading:
- **The two winners are the SHORTEST clips (18-20 s)** and the only ones with BOTH a high stay (~47%) AND high completion (78-90%).
- **High stay alone isn't enough:** galaxy (48%) and bolt_sort2 (51%) hooked people, but they left midway (52-63% viewed) because the clips were 32-38 s.
- **High completion alone isn't enough:** ring_escape, maze, laser and beam_clash have 98-132% viewed (rewatches), but only 26-31% stayed past the swipe, so the hook failed.

**Targets for new clips:** 15-20 s; stayed ≥ 47%; average viewed ≥ 85%. One instantly readable game rule; the payoff before people drift. The loser's fate can vary (doesn't have to be lava).
