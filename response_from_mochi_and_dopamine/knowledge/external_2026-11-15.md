## 0. What changes our plan (read this if nothing else)
1. **Shorts ad money is gated at 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days from 2027-02-01, even for channels already in YPP (A, verified).**
   - At 3 Shorts/day, 10M per 90 days means a 37K mean per Short. Mochi's mean is 10.4K (median 2.1K); DopamineEXE's is 2.6K.
   - A 300K Short buys subscribers and audience, not ad money.
   - Long-form hours are the paid route, and the new-entry bar doubles on the same date: 4K → 8K hours, 10M → 20M Shorts views (A).
   - **We have 78 days to enter YPP under the old bar.**
2. **Every 2026 peer that graduated did it fast and through a fixed wrapper**, not by slow growth:

| Channel | First 100K+ |
|---|---|
| How Big, Really? | day 13 |
| Ley Cero | day 17 |
| Satisfying Physics Sim | day 14 |
| Earclacks | day 16 (1M) |
| Kawaken | day 6 (1M) |

   - All of them found it within ~40 uploads.
   - DopamineEXE has 40 Shorts and no 100K. Its problem is the wrapper, not volume.
3. **DopamineEXE's lane now has direct one-person code competitors launched in Oct 2026**, with 29-72K medians:
   - Watch It Simulate (light around planets, metronomes, black holes);
   - How Big, Really?;
   - Hello Physics ("I coded a black hole");
   - Ley Cero.

   What they share and DopamineEXE lacks:
   - a famous object in almost every title;
   - ONE wrapper;
   - 18-26 s length;
   - the 💀 / black-hole register.

   DopamineEXE's 26/40 "Every X … until Y" titles read as a template, and they score z −0.05.
4. **Characters CAN create fandom from abstract balls when they are fighters with one ability and stats.** This challenges the prior "characters add cost, no benefit".
   - Earclacks' "weapon balls" have a Know Your Meme entry, fan theme songs (612K top), fan "leagues" and impersonator channels.
   - 14.5M top, 209K subs.
   - Mascot characters don't do this; fighters do.
5. **"AI slop" is now a reach risk, not just a comment.**
   - YouTube surveys viewers ("Does this feel like AI slop?", since 2026-03-17; B).
   - Its detector throttled Kurzgesagt in Jul 2026 because flat vector animation resembles slop (B): the worst upload in 13 years despite above-average CTR.
   - Our flat 2D blobs share that look.

### "AI slop" perception (our comments already say it)
- **Policy, verified (A, Help 1311392):**
  - Not monetisable: "Videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome" and "content that looks like it's made with a template".
  - Allowed: "a series following a set of characters across episodes" where "each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept".
  - Halprin (A, TechCrunch 2026-07-20): generic content "can be easily made with AI, CGI or templates". This is NOT limited to generative AI.
- **Enforcement so far hit:** photoreal generative AI, deception (fake trailers, fake crime), borrowed IP/religion, and huge templated volume.
  - Screen Culture (1.4M) and KH Studio were terminated Dec 2025.
  - CuentosFacinantes (5.9M) and others were wiped Jan 2026 (B).
  - No code-animated physics channel has been reported hit (B).
  - Collateral damage on faceless human channels is reported (B, TNW).
- **Detection risk:** Kurzgesagt was throttled by an automated slop detector in Jul 2026 (B). The "Does this feel like AI slop?" survey has been live since 2026-03-17; how it is used is undisclosed (B).
- **Audience:** an AI label lowers perceived effort (Frontiers 2026, n=618; B).
- **How peers handle it (M/B):**
  - Earclacks has a public process (Scratch profile, Discord) and fans who defend it.
  - Hello Physics' title "I Coded a Black Hole…" uses the making as the hook.
  - No data shows a process signal changes reach (unconfirmed).
- **Mitigations (I):**
  1. Never ship two videos with the same outcome or structure in a row, so each has a nameable change (rule 2).
  2. A visual signature that generative AI does badly: exact counters that add up, hard geometry, a seed stamp in the pinned comment.
  3. A "how it's made" Short every ~20 uploads (code → frame).
  4. Don't argue with accusers (owner rule).
  5. If a video gets a Kurzgesagt-type drop (normal CTR/APV, no impressions), record it. There is no fix path for small channels (vidIQ via Creator Handbook).

### Made for kids
- **Factors listed:** "animated characters or cartoon figures", "simple songs or games". But "animated content that appeals to everyone" is general-audience (A, Help 9528076).
- **Classifier:** an ML override cannot be edited, only appealed (B). Sonic fan animators were auto-flagged in 2022, with appeals "rejected almost immediately" (D).
- **FTC v. Disney ($10M, 2025):** the violation was a channel-level default. The lesson is a per-video audience decision (A).
- **Peer contrast:** NefiSims' "so cute" comments are the textbook risk. Weapon/rivalry/space-doom (💀) framing skews older (I).
- **Keep:** the CLAUDE.md MFK rules. Add a deliberate per-video "No, not made for kids" in Studio at upload.
