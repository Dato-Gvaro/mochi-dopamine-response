# Upload times for Shorts (2026-10-16)

Goal: catch US doomscrollers. The owner schedules in local time (a fixed offset, no DST).

## Facts (sourced)
- YouTube (as quoted by Shopify; primary page not found): publish time does not shape long-term performance, but it can affect early views, which can lead to more recommendations.
- Third-party studies conflict:
  - Buffer, 1.8M videos: Shorts best at 4/6/7 PM local, Fri or Wed.
  - SocialPilot, 301K videos: Shorts 12-3 PM and 7-9 PM, Fri 4-7 PM best.
  - All of them say to use your own Studio "When your viewers are on YouTube" chart.
  - Quality is weak: vendor blogs.
- Phone use peaks 18:00-23:00 local and is lowest at 05:00 (24 h passive tracking, students: PMC13448651).
- Gen Z is most likely to scroll for hours late at night (Morning Consult 2024).

## Measured (ours)
- 94% of views come from the Shorts feed.
- Top countries: PH 3.3K, US 2.8K, ID 2.6K, GE 2.0K, JP 1.9K, BR 1.7K.
- A Short gets most views on day 1, then ~40% of that on day 2 and ~15% on day 3. The first hours ARE the seed test.
