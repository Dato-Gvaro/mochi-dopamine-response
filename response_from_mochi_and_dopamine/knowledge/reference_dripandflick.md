### 1. Popcorn: "Flick heats 500 KERNELS / Can he pop ALL 500?" (1588, 13 s)
- **Mechanic:** a glass pot of kernels sits over Flick, who is the burner. Kernels pop into
  white puffs that take up more room.
- **Hook (0-2 s):** the first kernels pop at 0.5 s. The counter reads 5 at 0.5 s and 23 at 2 s.
- **Escalation:** the pop rate accelerates: 42 at 3 s, 120 at 6 s, 264 at 9 s, 391 at 10.5 s.
  The pot temperature readout climbs from 180 to 206 °C. The lid lifts at about 7.5 s, puffs spill
  over the rim at 9 s, and at 10.5 s the pot walls burst outward under a mountain of popcorn.
- **Payoff:** 500/500 at 12.9 s, and Flick closes his eyes, content. No loop.
- **Style:** a warm, dark night kitchen with a moon window, an amber glow from the flame, and a
  static camera. The pot fills about 60% of the width.
- **Why it satisfies:** a visibly accelerating chain, and **the container fails** (the lid lifts,
  the walls burst). The clip is short, so the counter finishes exactly as promised.

### 2. Frost solo: "Drip touched the ICE / Can the frost freeze the WHOLE screen?" (1445, 19 s)
- **Mechanic:** a branching frost network (DLA/L-system-like) grows out from Drip.
- **Hook:** Drip falls in from the top at 0.5 s, lands mid-screen at 1 s, and spikes sprout at 1.5 s.
- **Escalation:** coverage reads 1% at 3 s, 6% at 6 s, 26% at 10.5 s, 49% at 13.5 s and 79% at

- **Payoff:** "Frozen: 100%" at 19.3 s, with the whole screen frosted blue. No loop.
- **Style:** a navy-to-black gradient and an ice-blue line network. It is one colour and very calm.
- **Why it satisfies:** fractal growth that fills the space, plus a percentage that must
  reach 100.

## Synthesis: patterns behind the top performers

1. **A "reach 100%/ALL N" accumulation with an accelerating rate.** The top 3 are all this kind of
   clip (popcorn 1588, frost 1445, frost-vs-fire 1312). The counter visibly hits the stated number.
2. **The container or space FAILS at the climax.** In popcorn (1588), the lid lifts and the pot
   bursts. In frost (1445), the screen whites out. The finish is visually louder than the start.
3. **Short.** The three most-viewed clips are 13, 19 and 21 s. Clips of 32-42 s sit at 670-1267.
4. **Moving by 0.5 s, with no countdown or title card.** Every clip above 1k is moving at 0-0.5 s.
   The only countdown opener (#9) is the worst character video (670).
5. **One big, frame-filling object.** The pot, the helix, the tile island and the field fill
   0.85-0.95 of the width. The letterboxed 16:9 clip (#9, 670) and the thin bare circle (#11, 109)
   are the two floors.
6. **A rich, dark stage with glow.** The settings are a deep purple starfield, a night kitchen with
   a moon window, and neon tubes on violet. The flat-black minimal clip got 109.
7. **The characters ARE the mechanism.** Flick is the burner and Drip seeds the frost. They are
   inside the physics rather than standing next to it (#1-#5 are all 1.2k+), and they react only at
   the end (a content closed-eye face, or a sad face for the loser).
8. **The HUD narrates the swings.** It shows "LEAD: Drip +1/tied", plus "LEAD CHANGE!", "PHOTO
   FINISH!", "MATCH POINT", "1 vs 1!" and "OUT!" banners. Every competitive clip (1055-1267) uses
   these, so the story is readable with no sound.
9. **Engineered close finishes, stated in the title.** Results were 3-2, 20-19, 23-22, 53-47 and
   "by 0.93 s". The title "6 marbles, 1 point decides it" (1198) promises the closeness.
10. **A ladder counter as the spine.** Examples are "ring 9/22", "Stack 16/30", "Popped 264/500",
    "Frozen 49%" and "Rally: 13 hits". Every clip above 1k has one.
11. **Material variety per rung.** The helix rings (#4) and the marble course zones (#6) change
    material or mechanism every 2-3 s. This matches our owner's "every event unique" rule.
12. **Caveat on our own rules:** none of them loops, the pancake rate is flat, and they still
    reach 1.2k. On a channel this young, that says little against our loop and climbing-rate rules.
