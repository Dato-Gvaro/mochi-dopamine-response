# Hooks: winning the first 2 seconds (2026-10-09)

> **Update 2026-10-13:** the payoff-first REWIND (rule 2) is superseded. The owner dislikes reverse openings. Use a MID-ACTION frame 0 (something already on a collision course), see research/scroll_stop.md.

**Why:** a Short gets a ~1-3K-view seed test. The swipe decision in the first 1-2 s (Studio: "Stayed to watch" / viewed vs swiped) decides whether it is pushed wider. Rough bands: below 30% dies, about 50% is average, 70%+ goes wide. Our first 9 Shorts: 24-51%, all stuck at ~1.1K (reports/2026-10-09_channel_analysis.md).

## Rules for every new scene
1. **Frame 0 is the most striking frame in the video, or nearly.**
   - It must be a full, bright, readable picture, never an empty arena with a dot.
   - Show the PAYOFF, not the setup: a viewer decides from the first frame, without sound.
2. **Payoff-first cold open (preferred for builds that start small).**
   - Open ON the finished spectacle: the full mandala, the bell curve, the full bloom.
   - Within ~0.5-1.0 s it collapses, rewinds or dissolves to the start, then builds back up to it.
   - This fits our loop: the clip ends on the payoff, so the seam is payoff → payoff. It also plants the question "how does it get there?".
3. **Motion at frame 0 and a visible EVENT by 0.5 s.**
   - That means a hit, a burst, a ray converging, or a note firing.
   - Nothing slow-fades in. No static hold.
4. **Music hook at 0.0 s.** The most recognisable bar of the piece plays first. Never open on a quiet intro bar or a lone pickup note.
5. **The caption reads in one glance** (5-8 words for line 1). The stake must be visible before the first swipe impulse. Keep the persistent 2-line caption.
6. **Big subject.** The phenomenon fills 0.85-0.9 of the width from frame 0. Small detail is fine LATER, never at the open.
7. **Brightness / contrast.**
   - Our best three openings (bolt_sort2, paint_battle, hourglass_race: 48-51% stayed) were bright, with big characters and one-glance goals.
   - The worst three (ring_escape2, maze_pirate, seesaw_lava2: 24-31%) were dark, with small characters in busy machines.
   - Dark stages are allowed, but the frame-0 subject must be BRIGHT and large against them. This is a hypothesis (n=9) and gets re-checked with the new type's data.
8. **A pattern break every 2-3 s:** a new rung, colour change, count jump or zoom. If the viewer can predict the next 3 s, they swipe.

## Self-check before delivery
- Read frames 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 s of the ENCODED clip.
- At thumbnail size (~200 px wide), is frame 0 attractive and readable on its own? If it is mostly empty dark space, fix the opening.
