## Lessons for us
1. **Recognisable real-life situation + counter + escalation + twist** beats an abstract rule, *when the situation is a known meme*.
2. **Characters alone don't do it.** Our Mochi & Bolt duels: 1–4K. Our press series (no story): 25–234K.
3. **Made-for-kids risk:** cute cartoon cats with "so cute" comments are textbook MFK. Comments are still on, so they are not flagged yet.
4. **Money:** at ~$0.05/1K for Shorts, 47M views ≈ low thousands of $. Their long-form gets 11–59K views.
