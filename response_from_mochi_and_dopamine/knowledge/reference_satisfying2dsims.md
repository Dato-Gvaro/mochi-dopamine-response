## The formula (read from the frames of their top 4)
1. **One dead-simple rule, stated as caption line 1**, e.g. "Every ball CRUSHED splits into 3 SMALLER ones". Line 2 is a stake question: "How many can it HANDLE?" Two to three highlighted words.
2. **Starts with ONE ball and a threat already moving.** Frame 0 = 1 ball + a hydraulic press mid-stroke. The rule fires within ~2-3 s (first crush → 3 balls). They do NOT open on the payoff; the tension of "1 ball vs a press" is the hook.
3. **Exponential escalation with a giant counter.** "Balls: 1 → 3 → 85 → 229 → 15,939". Huge numbers in titles (15,939; quintillion; 1,152,921,504,606,846,976).
4. **Destruction/pressure physics:** crush, split, shatter, burn and extinguish; machines (hydraulic press, drop hammer).
5. **Minimal look:** neon outline arena on pure black, one counter, no characters, no scenery, no HUD clutter. Readable at 200 px.
6. **Long:** the top hits run 45-50 s (one is 15 s). Escalation keeps people to the end; the payoff is the final explosion of count.
7. **Franchises:** winners are repeated with one change (press → drop hammer; "splits into 3" ×2; fire vs water ×3).
8. **Two-sided contests also work** when the sides are RULES, not characters: "Fire SPREADS, water SPLITS — which runs out FIRST?" (1M).
9. **Titles:** "Can …?" / "How many …?" (94 of 297 titles contain "can"); "Every X does Y"; a number result.

## Gap vs our channel
- We: characters, scenery, multiple HUD panels, orchestral music, 20-30 s, payoff-first openings.
- They: one rule, one counter, abstract neon, exponential count, 45-50 s, tension-first openings.
