# murmuration2 — two hunters dive one by one at 15,000 starlings (batch25)

Rework of `murmuration` (owner 2026-10-10: "make them dive one by one; the monotone music sucks, the melody itself is good"). New file; `murmuration.py` untouched.

- **Interpretation of "them":** Mochi AND Bolt are both small winged hunters taking turns, one dive each, alternating (v1 had only Bolt diving). Mochi = STEEP: climbs above the aim and stoops near-vertically through the cloud. Bolt = LOW + WIDE: swings in from the lower right under the belly and sweeps across, exits left, returns low over the reeds.
- **Stake:** caption `2 hunters vs 15,000 starlings | ~Who gets CLOSEST?~`. Scoreboard "CLOSEST MISS" (MOCHI | BOLT, the leader in gold). At each impact a white ring (the clearance disc) and the measured miss in cm pop next to the hunter. The lead flips every dive (128 -> 114 -> 100 -> 88 -> 76 -> 66 cm); the climax is a DOUBLE dive from both sides at once: the flock splits, the two hunters BONK into each other (dizzy stars), Mochi wins 52 cm vs 58 cm, Bolt annoyed stare.
- **Honesty:** nobody is caught. Each dive has a hard clearance disc (64 -> 26 px) that birds are pushed out of; the miss shown is the MEASURED min 2-D distance of any bird during the dive (= the disc radius when birds pile against it; logged by `--report`). 1 px = 2 cm (a starling ~ 10 px).
- **Physics:** 3-D topological boids (7 nearest neighbours via cKDTree; align 1.8, cohesion 0.30, separation 1400 inside 13 px; noise 16), fixed dt 1/60, seed 3, 15,000 birds, V0 190 px/s.
  - The roost is a wide thin RIBBON (half-width 175 px middle -> 30 % at the ends, half-thickness 30 px) along a writhing 3-D spine (half-length 400, y swing 110, z swing 160; periods 13 / 11 s) that twists (periods 14 / 11.5 s) and turns (yaw/pitch 0.5 rad, 16 / 12.5 s). Bound force K 7.0 beyond r 0.80; an internal conveyor (one face flows +T, the other -T, weight 0.7) keeps the birds rolling. `--tph` = roost phase (2.0 picked: the end shape = frame 0 is the big S-swoop with a dark fold).
  - Lesson: a single bent/rippled ellipsoid sheet only ever projects to blobs and crescents; the twisting ribbon gives the iconic folding shapes. The roost must move slower than the birds (first try at ~150 px/s fragmented the flock).
  - Escape wave as v1: strike radius 100 -> 235 px, flee accel 900 -> 2100, alarm hops neighbour-to-neighbour (every 2 steps, x0.978 per hop, refractory 1.2 s).
- **Look:** sunset sky (violet -> rose -> gold, sun on the horizon, reeds). The flock is a smooth DENSITY SILHOUETTE: birds splatted (3 samples along heading) -> fine (sigma 0.75 px) + wide (sigma 4.4 px) density -> ink alpha `1 - exp(-(2.2 fine + 8 wide))`, so face-on parts read light and folds read black. Alarmed birds catch the sunlight: additive warm glow (bloom) replaces their ink — the escape wave is a bright ripple through the dark cloud.
