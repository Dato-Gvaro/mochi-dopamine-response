# vacuum_race — Mochi vs Bolt vacuum their half of a marble floor (NEW format)

- Rule: a plank floor (x 66..1014, y 352..1120) split by a rail. Each half holds 120 glossy marbles (8x15 grid, +-8 px seeded jitter, seeded colours). Each character IS the vacuum. Any marble inside its suction ring swirls into its open mouth, rides a clear hose down into its glass tank (below the floor, y 1178..1350), and drops onto the pile. The first half that is CLEAN wins. Counters "MOCHI n" / "BOLT n" at 0.775h show marbles sucked up; the leader's counter is bigger.
- Paths: robot-vacuum lanes, bottom to top. There are 7 horizontal lanes joined by semicircular U-turns, so both characters finish at the top of the divider, face to face. Lane widths grow geometrically (g per side from the seed, 1.06..1.20). Suction radius = lane half-width / 0.52. The ring steps up during each U-turn, with a small overshoot pump. Speed is v = 112 + 2.8t px/s (112 -> ~168). History: v1 opened at 40 px/s and was flagged as a swipe risk; v2 used 95 -> 111. v3 follows the owner's "a little more speed" and runs 7 lanes so the race still lasts ~20 s. Lean into turns is low-passed (0.12 s), so nothing jerks. Character r 50, marble r 14.
