# tower_smash

- NEW format (2026-10-04). Mochi (left) and Bolt (right) each work a crane on one shared beam. The crane swings a wrecking ball from directly above that character's own 5x13 block tower (65 blocks, 44x40). Each forward swing (inner side to outer side) scoops off the top. Every block the ball touches flies outward, tumbles off the plinth and lands on the floor; it scores +1 when it leaves the plinth. The first to knock off all 65 wins.
- Nameable rule: **every hit makes the ball BIGGER**. r = 32 + 1.3 per hit (cap 62). The ball grows with an overshoot "pump" during the catch dwell, with a soft pop. Swing period 1.32 -> 0.92 s and dwell 0.36 -> 0.10 s over 22 hits, so the swings also speed up.
- Physics: the ball is a real pendulum in x (x'' = -w^2 x along the cable circle). Each block it touches costs SMASH_C/r^2 = 420000/r^2 px/s of forward speed. Below 140 px/s the ball bounces back out. So a small ball takes 1-3 blocks and a big one rips through 8-10: the hit size escalates on its own. The ball is caught at its inner turning point, pulled back to x = AX, lowered to the next aim and released. Unsupported blocks fall down their column and land with a tok. Debris is ballistic with a tilted spin: it bounces on the plinth and floor, settles flat and slides out of frame.
- Aim: `clear_L` binary-searches the deepest cable that misses every block. The swing goes D = r*U(0.6, 1.5) deeper than that. 8% of swings (from swing 3) are WHIFFS: 6-12 px above the clear line, so no hit and no growth. Mochi's swing 0 is a forced whiff (the hook): Bolt laughs at 0.27 s while Bolt's first hit is already flying.
- Geometry: lanes x 292/788, half width 243 (0.9 w). Beam at y 452, pulleys at y 498. Plinth top 1250, floor 1330, counters at 1446/1528. AX 178 keeps the ball clear of the tower at both turning points for r <= 62.
- Reactions: on a whiff the other laughs (HA drawn outward, below the caption) and the whiffer winces (GAP 1.4). Grin on a >= 6-block swing. At the win: the loser goes wow, then gives an annoyed stare at the camera; the winner laughs 1.35 s, then cheers. Confetti comes from the cleared plinth. "BOLT WINS!" at y 1060, at +0.6 s. After the win both cranes lift and hang the balls straight (0.8 s).
- Loop: leftovers sink into the plinth, the full towers rise out of it and the counters count back. The balls arc (+70 px lift) onto swing 0 run backwards (`ball_pre`, with a +DT offset because sim frame f holds the state at (f+1)*DT). Pre-encode last->frame 0 mean 0.52 vs frame 0->1 0.75, so it is a normal motion step. The encoded seam mean of 0.97 is the ball's own motion.
- Sound: mountain_king (PD Grieg, classical) note per hit. Wooden pok per landing, capped at one per 50 ms per lane. Soft pop on each growth. Cheer arpeggio + pop. Foley is nudged +12.5 ms off the notes. No reset sound.
- Search: `--search N` runs 4 lane sims per seed (~15 s/seed). It keeps races of 19.5-28 s where the loser has 1-6 blocks left and no loser hit after the win. Score = lead changes, closeness, monotone offs/5 s.
