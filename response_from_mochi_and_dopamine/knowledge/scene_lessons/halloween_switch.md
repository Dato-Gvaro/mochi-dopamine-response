# halloween_switch
- Halloween special, the owner's own idea: day -> sudden night -> day again, but they are traumatised. A 22.07 s story Short (1324 frames). No seed: fully scripted, no RNG in the timeline. The scatter (stars, flowers, fireflies, bolt path) is seeded internally.
- DAY 0-7.17 s: Mochi (x300) and Bolt (x780) bounce on two springy red toadstools (cap surface y1185, contact 0.14 s, cartoon gravity 3600). One Vivaldi Spring note per boing. 4 sync boings (note + octave chord), then alternating double time (gaps 0.66 -> 0.44 s). Bolt does a high flip bounce at 2.4 s. Both launch together at 6.62 s on a 420 px full-flip jump.
- SWITCH 7.17 s (apex): they freeze mid-air with big eyes. Darkness spreads out of the castle's top tower as a radial ring wipe (0.8 s, band 260 px, no flash), preceded by a purple omen spark at 6.77 s. Each element morphs by its local ring value: birds -> bats (wing points lerp, ears, fangs, red eyes); castle grows spiky concave roofs, thorns, pointed merlons, glowing orange windows and a fanged gate; sun -> big moon behind the castle; trees lose their canopy and grow twisted branches; pumpkins become glowing jack-o'-lanterns; caps turn violet with glowing teal spots; fog and fireflies appear.
- NIGHT: Bach's Toccata mordent on organ at the switch. They drop at 7.92 s, and the run lands on D plus a D-minor chord exactly at the landing thud (8.41 s). Then they shiver to the Mountain King on pizzicato, 26 notes accelerating 0.30 -> 0.13 s, one shiver jolt per note. Each night event is unique: a bat swoops between them (9.2 s), a bat circles Mochi's head (10.85 s), a small lightning bolt behind the castle with a soft rumble (12.25 s; glow area under 10% of the frame), and a swarm of 8 bats flies out of the castle at the camera while they duck (13.25 s).
- DAY RETURNS 15.30 s: a 0.5 s ring wipe from the sun. The birds sing Spring with note glyphs; a bird lands on Bolt's head (16.4 s). Both stand frozen: eyes x1.62, tiny pupils that tremble, under-eye bags, wobbly mouth, Mochi's eye twitches (17.45 s), Bolt sweats (18.25 s). The music ends at 19.65 s, followed by a silent stare.
- LOOP: they blink (21.2 s), switch to happy eyes, shake it off (21.36-21.88 s), the bird is startled off, and they squash into the exact frame-0 boing state. Everything periodic (birds, clouds, wings, sun rays, stars, fog) completes a whole number of cycles per clip.
- Sound: the sidecar holds only the marimba Spring notes. Organ, pizzicato, chirps, bat flutters, rumble, thud, soft boings and the twitch tick are a scene-synthesised `bed`, so `tools/regen_audio.py` would drop them; rebuild with the scene instead. No drones, no harsh noise.
- Checks (v1): h264 yuv420p 1080x1920 60 fps, 22.07 s. -14.00 LUFS, peak -7.4 dBFS, last 0.5 s at -90.3 dBFS. flash_check PASS (1 flash, at the return wipe). Encoded seam mean 1.61, which check_clip flags as "broken" (>1.1), but this scene's normal frame step is 1.4-2.9 (median 1.74): the seam is an ordinary step (pre-encode frame0-1 1.218 vs 1323-0 1.227). Day boing rate climbs from 1.7/s to 3.7/s and Mountain King from 3.3 to 7.7 notes/s. Whole-clip events/5 s [17, 7, 2, 11] (marimba only) dip by design, because the night music lives in the bed.
