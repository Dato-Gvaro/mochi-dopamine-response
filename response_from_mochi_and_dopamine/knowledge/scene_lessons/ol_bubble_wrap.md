- New scene, character-free (docs/BRIEF_channel2.md, docs/BRIEF_overnight_1023.md). Skeleton: a chain reaction (every popped bubble pops its neighbours), the popped count doubling every 2.16 s (×54) toward a named end state; one concrete object (a sheet of bubble wrap), one obvious rule.

## Rule / model (exact, no integration)
- Chain ORDER: first-passage (Dijkstra) from the centre bubble over the 6 hex neighbours; each neighbour's fuse is seeded 1 ± 0.45, so the front is ragged-round and ONE corner bubble is last.
- Chain CLOCK = the music: rank k pops at t_k = D·log2(k / N0) (N0 = 27 popped at frame 0, D = 2.163 s = the count doubles every 2.16 s), snapped UP onto the 16th grid; rank N−1 on the 16th before the cadence, the last bubble alone ON the cadence. A trigger always has a lower rank, so it never pops after the bubble it set off.
- v1-v3 last bubble: the bottom-left corner (151, 1191). **v4: the last bubble is stubborn #7** (see below); the natural corner bubble pops with the final crowd (12.455 s). The v1-v3 gold marker ring is gone (the itch works unannounced).
- **v4 stubborn bubbles (`--nostub` = v3):** 7 bubbles the front leaves intact inside the popped film; each gives in on a score DOWNBEAT and the next has already been left behind elsewhere. Picked by `pick_stubborn` (seeded): natural pop time near the plan's target, ≥ 4 neighbours popped within 0.5 s of it (#1: all neighbours popped by frame 0), every neighbour popped ≥ 0.8 s before it gives in, inside the window with a 3-radius margin, ≥ 3 pitches from the others. While waiting it is drawn on top, 1.0 → 1.32× radius as the camera pulls back (still reads at full-sheet scale), and shivers +6 % on each beat. Giving in: the normal flatten/flick/crinkle + a small calm double ring (dome hue mixed with pale gold, 2.7 r, alpha 0.50/0.30, 0.5 s; no flash) + a bigger snap (two layered snaps ×1.7 / ×1.0 + a 7-snap crinkle tail over 150 ms).
- Schedule (left behind → pops, screen pos when left): #1 frame 0 (natural −2.28) → 2.95 s (bar 3 downbeat) at (441, 650); #2 1.74 → 4.55 at (727, 820); #3 3.48 → 6.16 at (271, 629); #4 5.22 → 7.77 at (382, 548); #5 6.96 → 9.38 at (410, 993); #6 8.57 → 10.98 at (803, 720); **#7 9.91 → 12.59 = the cadence, the sheet's last bubble, alone** at (544, 1074).
