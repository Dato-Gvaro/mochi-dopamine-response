# siege_duel: Catapult vs Trebuchet, first to BREACH the wall (batch30)

- **Format (choice duel):** Mochi CHOSE a torsion CATAPULT (mangonel); Bolt CHOSE a counterweight TREBUCHET. Two identical stone walls (7 x 11 blocks + 4 merlons, 0.5 m blocks, 3.5 m thick, 6 m tall), same distance, same physics. Bolt's field on top (dusk plateau), Mochi's on the lower terrace (escarpment backdrop).
- **World:** MEDIEVAL only (bridge_test3's dusk palette): stone, wood, rope, iron, purple-and-gold defenders' banners, torches, a far keep, a small dragon silhouette (2.4-11 s L->R, 13-20.2 s R->L, high in the sky).
- **Physics (SI, g 9.81, quadratic drag, dt 1/240 s, all seeded):**
  - Catapult: 1/2 m v^2 = eta E_spring, 3.6 kJ x 40 % -> 12 kg stone at 15.5 m/s, low flat arc (~15 deg), crew scatter sigma 0.55 m around 2.6 m.
  - Trebuchet: v = sqrt(2 g h M/m eta), 1.15 t falling 1.8 m, 40 %, 200 kg boulder -> 9.0 m/s at ~58-62 deg, ~17 kJ at impact (it falls from the 6.8 m release).
  - Impact: absorbed energy (1 - e^2, e 0.35) is poured into blocks around the contact (Gaussian, footprint 2r + 0.1 m); each block ignores the first 650 J of a blow (elastic) and breaks at 1.7 kJ absorbed. Leftover energy carries the projectile through the hole.
  - Support: a block stands on the block below or hangs from a held neighbour (1 block of mortar reach); statics: when the centre of mass of everything above a course lies outside that course's standing blocks, it topples = THE BREACH.
  - Boulders landing in the crater at the wall's foot smash into the toe (nearest intact block within r + 0.9 m). The crew re-lays each boulder onto the back of the crater it already dug (aim recomputed at launch - 0.8 s).
  - Launch times are calibrated (2 deterministic re-runs) so every shot's FIRST contact is on its beat (worst 1.8 ms).
- **Why the trebuchet wins (visible):** HUD "12 kg stone: 1.5 kJ" vs "200 kg boulder: 17.2 kJ" + a kJ popup per hit. The catapult's 31 stones mostly fall under the blocks' elastic threshold and spread over the face (10 % damage); the trebuchet's 3 hits all go into the same toe until the wall's weight sits past its support and the whole section topples.
- **Story (seed 1):** catapult leads early (3 % vs 0) -> trebuchet's first boulder is TOO SHORT (80 deg release, lands at 4.08 s) -> SLING ADJUSTED -> beat 20 hit: LEAD CHANGE (6 % vs 3 %) -> beat 32 hit 14 % -> catapult climbs to 10 % on a quickening one-stone-per-beat rate -> beat 48 (bar 170's climax chord) hit -> BREACH, the wall topples in slow motion (rate -> 0.08), banner falls, TREBUCHET WINS (beat 52). Bolt cheers, Mochi annoyed stare across the seam.
