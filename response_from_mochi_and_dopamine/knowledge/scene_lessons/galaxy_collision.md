# galaxy_collision — Mochi's galaxy vs Bolt's galaxy (batch23)

- Concept: two spiral galaxies (coral = Mochi's core, mint = Bolt's core), 10,000 stars each, pass, swing round, throw tidal tails and a bridge, and merge. Stake: whose core KEEPS more stars.
- Physics: restricted N-body (Toomre & Toomre 1972). Two Plummer-softened cores (eps 0.25) attract each other; stars are massless test particles (eps 0.08). KDK leapfrog, fixed dt 0.004 sim units, seeded star sampling. A dynamical-friction drag on the cores' relative velocity, `kdrag * exp(-(d/ddrag)^2)`, lets the pair fall back and merge after the first pass.
  - Disks: exponential-ish bulge (25 %) plus a uniform disk, 60 % of the disk stars on two trailing log-spiral arms (pitch 20°). Circular orbits at t = 0. Both prograde. Mochi's disk is nearly flat (2.6°); Bolt's is tilted 23°.
  - Chosen physics (`PHYS`, seed 347): m2 0.999, q 1.293, e 0.799, D0 4, kdrag 2.238, ddrag 0.819, inc (2.59°, 23.24°), om (2.23°, 163.36°).
  - Orbit: first pericentre tau 4.51 (sep 1.29), widest swing-out tau 6.38 (sep 1.48), lock tau 8.78 (sep 0.60), clip ends at tau 9.38 (cores merged).
- Count rule: a star is kept by the core it is bound to (two-body energy relative to that core < 0; the lower one wins); unbound = "lost" (gold). Display = 0.25 s centred moving average. The count LOCKS when the cores first come within 0.60 after the swing-out: on the final chord.
  - Timeline (clip s): 10,000 / 10,000 → Bolt ahead from the approach → **Mochi takes the lead 12.9 s** → **Bolt retakes it 18.6 s** → lock 20.0 s: **Mochi 8,400 vs Bolt 8,720, Bolt wins** (+320). Up to ~3,200 stars lost to the tails along the way.
  - Seed/physics found by `--search` (random physics on 1,500 stars/galaxy, orbit filter: q1 0.95-1.35, swing-out 1.15-1.8, lock 2.5-5.5 after the pass), then confirmed at the full 20,000.
- Time warp: PCHIP clip-time → sim-time through the music: start 0.67 s → tau 0; pericentre on the held E at 6.67 s; swing-out at 11.4 s; lock on the final chord at 20.0 s; end 23.98 s → lock + 0.6. Sim speed 0.95 on the approach, ~0.11-0.25 through the collision (slow motion).
- Hook: frame 0 = the last frame (merged cores, two long coral/mint/gold tails, final counts, Bolt grinning, Mochi's annoyed stare). Eased rewind of the whole history in 0.67 s (10 sub-samples a frame → motion blur), landing on the first long note, then two clean spiral galaxies approach.
