# cradle_duel — Mochi vs Bolt rally through a chrome Newton's cradle (NEW format)

- Rule: a 5-ball chrome cradle (ball r 44, wire 540, pivots y 420). Mochi and Bolt stand on steel stools exactly where the end ball's swing reaches (16 deg out). The ball that flies out hits the character there, who headbutts it back. It swings down, CLACKs through the row (1 in -> 1 out), and the far ball flies at the other character. Caption "Every hit gets HARDER|~First one knocked OFF loses~".
- Physics: the cradle is one "unfolded" pendulum (phi<0 = left ball out, phi>0 = right ball out; the momentum passes through the row at phi=0). theta'' = -(g/L) sin theta, g set for a 1.15 s period, 40 substeps per frame, 0.97 of the velocity kept per clack. A hit returns the ball at 0.40*v_in + P.
- Escalation (one rule): P = 300 + 720*(t/24)^2.2 with seeded noise (sigma 0.06). A harder push means a faster arrival, so the exchange time drops from ~0.7 s to ~0.19 s. Clacks+hits per 5 s: 31, 34, 42, 59.
- Knock-off: arrival > V_KO 1500 px/s. The receiver takes all the momentum (the ball stops dead) and flies up and out of frame. Hits from 0.82 of V_KO up make the receiver teeter back about its rear foot (worried face + sweat). The other one laughs at a near knock-off (u >= 0.86, gap 2.4 s, max 4).
- End: the ball swings back. On the next clack the winner lunges in and puts its face on the end ball, so the cradle stops dead at rest (the serve in reverse). The winner laughs, then cheers with confetti. "KNOCKED OFF!" plus "<NAME> WINS!" (y 1310). The loser hops back in from off-screen and stares at the camera, annoyed. The RALLY counter (y 1446/1530) counts back to 0.
- Loop: frame 0 = cradle at rest, Mochi mid-way through the serve headbutt (the lunge runs -0.15 to +0.10 s). The last 0.15 s replays the lunge start. Pre-encode: last->frame-0 delta 0.61 = the frame-0->1 delta 0.62 (only Mochi moves). The encoded seam mean of 0.92 is that motion plus keyframe drift, not a jump.
- Sound: an in-scene steel CLICK per clack, synthesized with numpy (sub-ms contact transient plus 2.2-9.4 kHz steel partials, level from impact speed), passed as `bed`. Each clack also gets one note of mountain_king (Grieg, PD), so the tune speeds up with the rally (sync). In the 40 ms after a clack the click and the note measure equal RMS (around -19/-20 dB). A character hit = pok, nudged +12 ms off notes. Knock-off = bounce + pok + falling run 79-76-72-67. Cheer = 71-74-78-83 + pop. The serve plays at 0.10 s, so the default intro fill is off (a bed disables it).
- CAVEAT: the click bed is NOT in `_events.json`. tools/regen_audio.py would drop the clicks; re-render the scene instead.
- `--search N [--from S]` (sim only, ~5 ms/seed): keeps knock-offs at 21-24 s with >= 1 near knock-off (u >= 0.88) per side in the last 4 s. Ranks by near count + the winner's max u.
