# maze_duel — Mochi vs Bolt, ONE turning maze, two marbles, two levers (NEW format, 2026-10-05)

- Built from the owner's review of maze_spin ("two mazes at once is too heavy: one bigger maze, two balls in the characters' colours at two start positions, levers to push it"). maze_spin.py is untouched (s150 still reproduces).
- Rule: one round wooden board (disc r 460 = 0.85 of the width, centre 540/868) holds a 9x9 steel-wall maze (cell 68, walls 15, marbles r 22). It turns a quarter turn per beat. Mochi's coral marble and Bolt's mint marble start at two brass hatches the SAME number of turns from the centre hole (BFS, 13 each for s203). First marble in the hole wins. Caption "One maze, two marbles|~First one in WINS~". Counters = honest BFS turns left per marble, "N TO GO" -> "IN!".
- Levers: each character sits on a wooden shelf at a top corner (x 92 / 988, y 485) with a lever (pivot 192 / 888) and a shoe on the disc rim. On every beat both pick the best quarter turn for THEIR marble: BFS over turns + slides, with the other marble as an obstacle on the first slide, plus a visit penalty against cycles, and a 6% wrong pick. Same way (or one indifferent) = a SYNC push: both levers swing together and the board turns. Opposite = TUG: the levers strain (angry faces), the board shudders and holds (0.30 -> 0.18 s), then one push wins. The marble further behind wins the tug with p 0.62, else a coin (catch-up rule: strict alternation made 1/4 of the seeds cycle forever). Every turn rolls BOTH marbles, so every move helps one and may hurt the other.
- Gates: one per colour on that marble's best route (a +2..+5 turn detour), opened only when its OWN marble rolls over its coloured switch plate (click, lamp, gate retracts). A closed gate blocks both marbles.
- Physics: the board-frame tilt model from maze_spin (gravity turning, stiction MU_S 1.6 = rolls past ~58 deg, e 0.30, mu 0.06, g 3300, 8 substeps), plus marble-marble impacts (e 0.45, the two can block each other). A beat stays ARMED until both marbles rest (or the next beat comes). Skipping the beat made the turn rate fall at the end.
- Tempo: beat 1.35 -> 0.62 s (linear to 24 s), turn 0.4 beat (0.30-0.55 s). Turns/5 s to the win: 5, 5, 6, 7. Hits + seams/5 s: 15, 37, 31, 36.
- Start: the characters land a hop at frame 0. The hatches are already open (they open in the last 0.14 s of the pre-roll) and both marbles pop up out of them (0.18 s) with a pop + latch. The first turn is at 0.62 s.
- End: the marble sinks into the hole (0.24 s) with the metal-drop rattle and an arpeggio. Winner: cheer + confetti from its shelf + "<NAME> WINS!" (y 1400), then a laugh. Loser: wince, then an annoyed stare at the camera. The loser keeps turning alone until its marble is in. 1.0 s later the board turns home (nearest 2pi), the gates slide shut, the plates pop up and the counters count back. The silent hold then replays the 0.40 s crouch + hop.
- Sound: fur_elise marimba (PD), one note per marble per turn at its slide-end clack; on a synced turn the two notes land almost together. Ratchet per turn (centred on a sync, panned to the winner on a tug). Tug = thud + two strain clicks. Marble-marble = pok + heavy steel tick. Latch on plates and gate ends, servo slide on gates. Steel ticks per cell seam and per hit, plus the hole drop, sit in the metal bed with NO roll noise.
- Seed search `--search N [--from S]` (~12 min / 1200 seeds; most seeds fail the fair-start generator). It keeps t_w 19-26.5 s and loser <= 3.5 s behind. Score = lead changes*2 + tugs/2 - gap*0.4 + 2 for a Mochi win - 1 per loser turn left beyond 1. Alternatives: s464 (Mochi 25.93 vs 26.9, 6 lead changes, 14 tugs), s1190 (Mochi 21.44 vs 22.58), s736 / s91 (Bolt photo finishes).

Mochi's tug at 4.5 s sets Bolt back (Mochi laughs); Bolt's tug at 9.1 s sets Mochi back (Bolt laughs). Clip 27.40 s (1644 f).
