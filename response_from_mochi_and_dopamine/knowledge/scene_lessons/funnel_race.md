# funnel_race — Mochi vs Bolt, two gravity-well coin funnels (NEW format)

- Rule: two coin funnels side by side, seen from above (centres x 272/808, y 880; inner wall R 232, brass rim 16, throat R 22). Mochi sits on the top of the left rim and Bolt on the right one. At frame 0 each flicks its marble along the rim toward the screen centre. The left funnel runs clockwise and the right one counter-clockwise (mirror image). MOST LAPS WINS. A counter counts that marble's full laps from its launch point and freezes when the marble drops through the throat. Caption "Two funnels, one flick each|~Most laps WINS~". Counters at 1412/1500, the leader's count in yellow, a frozen count in grey. "<NAME> WINS!" at y 1268.
- Physics: a marble on the surface z = -A/r (GA 7.2e7, g 2500). r^2*theta' is conserved, the radial axis has the (1+z'^2) metric term, and drag is viscous 0.038/s plus rolling 3 px/s^2. Integrated with 16 substeps per frame. The orbit decays roughly as r ~ e^(-2ct), so the lap rate climbs exponentially: about 2 s per lap at the rim, 0.15-0.17 s at the throat. A marble tips in at r < 35 and then takes a 0.30 s fall: it shrinks, darkens and keeps whirling, then a white ripple shows at the hole. Depth cue: the marble's radius goes 18 -> ~11 with depth. Trails last 0.20 s and are capped at 0.85 of a lap, so they tighten into a ring.
- Chance: a seeded flick strength (0.86-1.02 x the circular speed) and a radial wobble (rd0 -45..15). The weaker flick spirals in sooner, LEADS on laps and drops first. The other marble then has to beat the frozen count in its final fast laps before it drops too. The total lap count barely depends on the launch, so finishes are naturally close.
- v1 used ONE shared funnel with marble-marble collisions. In almost every seed a rear-end hit knocked one marble in 5-10 s early (8-16 laps vs 30+), and one seed rode in contact for 75 hits. Split into two funnels.
- Search `--search N [--from S]` (~10 s per 1500 seeds): keeps t2 19.5-25.5 s, a 1-lap margin and a 0.6-3.0 s gap between the drops. Scores favour a 1.0-2.2 s chase and a survivor who comes back to win. Every passing seed in the top list is a survivor comeback.
