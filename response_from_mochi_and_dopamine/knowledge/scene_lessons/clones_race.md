# clones_race — Mochi clones vs Bolt clones, every landing multiplies

- From mochi_clones after the owner's review ("both together and compete"). Two identical plinko halves side by side (Mochi left, Bolt right) beat one shared board, because mixed colours on one board hide who is ahead. Bins are [x2 x3 x5 x3 x2]; every bin multiplies, so there is no x1. First to 1000 wins.
- Counter = clones MADE. It jumps at the landing (+m). The m new clones queue and enter the top of their half through a pipe at R(t) = 8 + 1.2t per second. R(t) is the SAME for both halves and depends on time, not on a side's own count. That makes the landing rate climb to the last frame, and the lead is decided by bin luck: x5 hits cause the late swings. A cap based on each side's own count would feed back and let the leader run away.
- v1 (2 starts, x1 bin, a fixed 0.035 s cap, first to 200) failed the opening: 2-7 clones per side for the first 4 s. In the second half both sides were also capped and climbed in lockstep. v2 starts 10 clones per side mid-fall, one between each pair of peg rows (y = 602 + 60k), so the first landing comes at 0.23 s and about 12 clones per side are visible at 0.5 s.
- Each half is its own physics2d World, so a run depends only on its own seed. `--search N` simulates N single-half runs once (light mode, about 6 s each, run in a pool, cached in output/clones_race_pool.json) and scores every (m, b) pair. The filter keeps a finish gap of 6-50 and ranks by how late the last lead change is.
- Physics: g=800, clone MR=16, 10 peg rows, 6/5 pegs per row, edge pegs 50 px off the wall. At 34 px off the wall, clones wedged between the peg and the wall.
- Lead = strict count lead that holds 0.5 s, back-dated to when it began, so the crown flips with the counter. Reactions: the new leader laughs and the old leader looks worried; far behind = worried; at the finish the winner cheers twice and the loser is annoyed. The cheer jump is scaled by 0.55 so it clears the caption.
- Render: clones are pre-rendered sprites (open/blink). Up to about 280 live clones; a full FHD render takes about 1 min.
- Sound: one ode_to_joy note (+12) per landing, with a ceiling gap of 0.03 s; x5 hits always sound, with a pop. x3 gets a small pop (0.15 s gap). Peg pings use penta by row, gap 0.1 s, quiet. The finish plays an arpeggiated C-major chord at the winner's x. Tail landings sound for 1 s after the finish. The reset plays a pop with no note.
- Loop: the clones pop away, the counters count down to 10:10, the crown fades, and the start clones fade in at their frame-0 state. Pre-encode seam: max delta 0.
- Pick m16 b33: Mochi wins 1000:978 at 23.2 s, clip 26.6 s. 6 lead changes (11.4, 12.1, 16.7, 19.1, 20.6, 22.7); the last one comes 0.5 s before the finish, from Mochi x5 hits. Landings per 5 s up to the finish: [47, 145, 230, 316]. Sounded landings per second: 4 → 27 by 19 s, then about 25 up to the finish.
- Measured (m16 b33): -14.0 LUFS, peak -6.6 dB, last 0.5 s at -65.7 dBFS, flash_check 2 flashes = PASS, h264 yuv420p 60 fps 1080x1920, encoded seam mean delta 0.47.
