# maze_spin — Mochi vs Bolt, two turning tilt-mazes (NEW format, 2026-10-05)

- Rule: each character turns its own square wooden labyrinth a quarter turn at a time. Gravity stays put, so every turn tips the board and the steel marble rolls down the new corridor until it clacks into a wall. The hole in the centre cell is the goal. Caption "Turn the maze, roll to the CENTER|~First one in WINS~". Counters "N TO GO" = honest BFS turns-left (it goes UP after a bad turn), then "IN!".
- Look: 7x7 boards (cell 52.6, steel walls 10 thick, dark-wood frame, wood grain, faint cell seams), centres x 285 / 795, y 800. The brass-ringed hole is in the centre cell. Each board has one brass GATE plus a brass SWITCH PLATE with a lamp. Rolling over the plate gives a click and lights the lamp, then the gate retracts into the wall (0.26 s). The marble is chrome, r 17. Glass posts run to the floor cups (y 1212), with a brass launch button under each character (x 455 / 625). Theme sky.
- Layout rule: a board at 45 deg must clear the screen edge and the other board at rest: h(1+sqrt2) <= dx. So the turns NEVER overlap: the sides alternate on one beat, each turn is <= 0.4 beat, and the home turns run one after the other.
- Physics: integrated in the BOARD frame with gravity turning (g 2600, 8 substeps, capsule walls, e 0.30 over 90 px/s, mu 0.06). Stiction MU_S 1.6: a resting marble rolls only once its wall tips past ~58 deg. Why: real rotating-frame physics (the Euler/centrifugal fling of a 0.6 s turn, alpha*r ~ g) and early rolling into side openings made ~half the slides disagree with any plan, and the player cycled forever.
- Player: moves are planned on the real maze (BFS over quarter turns + discrete slides, gate state included). Both moves are scored 1 + BFS after the slide, plus a 0.6 penalty per earlier visit (no cycles). Ties go to a coin flip. A seeded wrong turn has P 0.10 when n > 2. - End: on IN the marble sinks into the hole (0.2 s), drops down the post into the cup (clack) and the metal-drop rattle plays.
