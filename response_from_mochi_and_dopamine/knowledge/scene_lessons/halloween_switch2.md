# halloween_switch2
- Built from the owner's review of halloween_switch (2026-10-06). Changes: birds -> butterflies, mushroom bounce -> butterfly-net catches, cartoon bolt-action rifles at night, and a cute stressed face. Imports halloween_switch's world (sky, castle, moon, trees, pumpkins, fog, ring wipes, bat, organ/pizz synths) and only re-times its module globals (D, SWITCH, T_DARK, T_RET, LIGHTNING). v1 stays bit-reproducible. 21.50 s (1290 frames), fully scripted, no seed.
- DAY 0-6.78 s: hop-run (run steps of 0.3 s) plus catch hops. The net is held on a body-coloured arm nub and drawn behind the body (handle 175 px, hoop r44: gold for Mochi, cyan for Bolt), with a translucent bag that trails the swing. Swing: wind-up, a sine sweep +-0.16 s through the hoop point, then follow-through. Each catch pulls the nearest free butterfly into the hoop (0.35-0.8 s, scaled by distance), holds it 0.32 s and releases it with a sparkle and a ting (no harm). Catch notes: Spring. 11 single catches (0.43 -> 6.01 s, alternating), then a SYNC double catch at the apex of a joint jump = SWITCH (chord).
- SWITCH: they freeze mid-air with cute shocked eyes, the castle ring wipe runs (v1), and the butterflies in the nets turn into bats and escape at +0.55 s. Toccata mordent; they land on the run's last D (7.89 s).
- NIGHT: POOF (8.19 s) turns the nets into rifles, then a sync rack (click-clack). Mountain King pizzicato 9.00-14.38 s, accelerating. Every even note is a shot, alternating Mochi/Bolt: pop sound, small muzzle star + glow (only ~4 frames), pellet streak 0.07 s, bat spins and shrinks into a lilac puff plus gold stars. No blood. After each shot: recoil, bolt-handle cycle (click +0.13 s, clack +0.32 s), a brass casing spins out. The 7 butterfly-bats are shot first. Lightning at 11.55 s. Then 7 swarm bats fly out of the tower toward the camera; the last two are shot in SYNC on the final note. Faces: determined; grin on a shot; wow at the lightning; sweat from the swarm on.
- DAY RETURNS 14.82 s: ring from the sun, the shot butterflies pop back in, POOF turns the rifles back into nets. Cute stressed face: glossy eyes with a big iris, two highlights, a 4-point sparkle and a wet rim; small worried brows; tiny wobbly mouth; light sweat; pupils tremble plus a 1.2 px body tremble. Spring plays again (sung by the butterflies). A butterfly lands on Bolt's sprout, they glance at each other (17.35 s), Bolt sweats. Silent hold, then a blink (20.45 s), shake-off (20.6-21.1 s), the butterfly leaves, and a crouch into frame 0.
- Rifles are held at the body side (pivot 0.92R out, 0.26R down) so they never cross the faces. The idle and rack angle is near-vertical, slightly outward: v2 had the barrels crossing between them and pointing at each other, which was fixed.
