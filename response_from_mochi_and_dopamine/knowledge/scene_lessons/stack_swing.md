# stack_swing

- EPISODE of stack_race (stack_race.py untouched). The ONE change: "this time the blocks SWING". Each lane's next block hangs from a crane rope and swings like a pendulum. The character on the crane beam lets go, the block drops straight down (12% of the swing speed carries), and the overhang is sliced off. Same rule as stack_race: first to MISS loses (< 16 px overlap).
- Swing: x = 115 sin(phi), with the phase continuous across levels. omega(k) = (2pi/1.4) * (1 + 2.6*((k-1)/75)^1.15), so it gets faster EVERY level with no cap (period 1.4 s at level 1, ~0.42 s at level 68). Amplitude 115 px + block 230 px = the lane's half width, so the two lanes' blocks never overlap at the divider. The block tilts with the rope and gets an arc lift of 18*(x/A)^2.
- Timing-error model (seeded, one RNG per lane): the player picks the ideal release time (the first crossing after a "watch" delay of 0.33 s -> 0.03 s), then slips by gauss(0, 7.5 ms). 7% of releases are a real slip of +-16-34 ms. A landing within 4 px snaps to Perfect. The same slip costs more as the swing speeds up, which is the escalation. 2 perfects in a row regrow the block by 14 px (same as stack_race). The next block clips on 0.08-0.15 s after a release.
- Fix for stack_race's weak point (small characters on tall towers): the characters sit on a crane beam at a FIXED screen spot (R 64 vs 40), so the faces stay big at any tower height. Their eyes follow their own swinging block, and they lean with the swing and squash on the let-go.
- Layout: caption 2 lines; beam y 500 with characters + VS on it; pivots y 548; lanes x 302 / 778, 460 wide. The camera holds the HIGHER top at y 948, and the rope lengths differ honestly per lane. Counters at 1446/1528 (0.75/0.80 h); altitude haze below 1330.
- Opening: both blocks already swinging at frame 0. Mochi lets go late (28 px slice, lands 0.53 s) and Bolt lands a perfect at ~0.7 s, so Bolt laughs at 0.65 s and Mochi winces (hook).
- Reactions (notable only): slice >= 17 px -> the other laughs (HA HA, drawn outward, clear of the caption) and the dropper winces. A 3rd perfect in a row while wider -> the other is worried. GAP 1.6 s. Miss: "MISS!" above the loser's top; the loser goes wow -> dizzy stars (1.5 s) -> annoyed stare at the camera. The winner laughs (1.35 s), then cheers + confetti + "<NAME> WINS!" (y 760). The winner's last block stays on the hook and the swings damp out.
- Loop: the towers sink into the plinths, the counters count back, the camera returns, and the hooks reel back. The frame-0 block fades in on the frame-0 swing phase run BACKWARDS from the clip end, so the seam is a normal motion step. Pre-encode, the last->frame-0 diff equals the frame0->frame1 diff (mean 0.60 vs 0.62). Post-encode, check_clip reports a seam mean of 0.83: that is the swing's own per-frame motion plus keyframe drift, not a jump.
- Sound: blue_danube (waltz, for the swing) note per landing; quiet pok; perfect = octave + small pop; slice = soft snap; miss = falling run 79-76-72-67 + bounce; cheer arpeggio + pop; hiss at the reset start. Notes are spaced >= 12 ms first, then each foley onset is nudged +12 ms off any note (the mixer drops onsets < 10 ms apart), so no note is ever dropped.
