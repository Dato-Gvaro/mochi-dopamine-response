# gear_machine - Mochi vs Bolt, crank-driven marble machines

- NEW format (2026-10-04). Each character turns a crank. Crank gear z24 (brass) -> idler z12 (steel, 2x speed) -> head gear z24 (copper) on a bucket ELEVATOR. 16 cups on the chain, cup pitch = half the head sprocket circumference, so 1 crank turn = 2 marbles (the caption's spec). The cups scoop steel marbles out of a hopper, lift them and tip them onto a rail. The rail feeds a swinging CHUTE that aims each marble at one of 7 metal bars (one bar per pitch of eine_kleine, PD Mozart). The bar rings, the marble drops through its gap and a funnel into the tank. First tank to the dashed fill line (60) wins. Caption "1 crank turn = 2 marbles|~First FULL tank WINS~".
- Exact mechanics: gear angles come from the mesh formula (a tooth of the driver on the centre line <-> a gap of the driven gear, rolling without slip), so teeth never slip or jitter. The chute angle per bar is solved so the marble leaves the tip ALONG the chute and meets the bar's shoulder after 0.13 s. The bounce (e 0.28, tangential 0.22) sends it into the gap beside the bar. Funnel slide, then fall with x easing onto its hex slot (8/7 per row).
- Sync: both characters crank to ONE shared tempo, offset by half a cup, so the bars ring L-R-L-R evenly and the melody (shared walker in chute-entry order) never reorders. Rate 2 -> ~4.4 cups/s per side (`rate()`, p 1.25 over 24 s). The melody speeds up with it.
- Chance = JAMS (`jam_plan`): a marble wedges at the head, that chain stops dead (7 px blend) and is held for EXACTLY m cups (2-4) of the shared tempo, so the L-R rhythm survives. The jammer is worried/determined and shakes, the jammed marble trembles, then it clears. The other laughs (gap 2 s). Jams decide every lead change.
- Finish: the counters FREEZE at the win (winner yellow). The pipeline holds ~6 marbles (dump -> land ~1.4 s), so both tanks keep filling past the line after the win; frozen counts carry the result. Both cranks brake to rest (constant decel, 0.16 s minimum) on the frame-0 crank angle (mod one crank turn). Winner cheers + confetti from its tank, then laughs. Loser winces, then gives an annoyed stare. Banner "<NAME> WINS!" at y 905.
- Loop: trap doors drop both tanks into the hoppers, the counters count down, the chute eases back to its frame-0 aim, and the last PRE = 1.0 s replays the sim's pre-roll (spin-up from rest). Hopper count = S0 + cups(-PRE) - cups(t) - dumped + absorbed, so it is exact at the seam.
