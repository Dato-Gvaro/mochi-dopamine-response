# tube_race — FILL THE TUBE (new format)

- Rule: one gumball dispenser feeds a 14-peg Galton field (4 rows, no single top peg). A centre wedge splits candies into Mochi's tube (left) or Bolt's (right). Candies settle into a hex pile with 17 rows of 6/5 = 94 slots, so the tubes ARE the progress bars. Slot choice is the lowest free row, then the slot nearest the entry x. A slot can't arrive before the slots it rests on. First tube FULL (94 landed) wins.
- Pour rate is 3.2 + 0.076*t^1.5 /s (~14/s at 27 s). Release vy is 650 with a 35 px spout clearance. GOTCHAS: a single top peg under the spout made candies bounce back into the spout zone and capped the rate at ~3/s. Candy mu > 0 damps sliding on walls (physics2d applies mu every substep), so candies use mu 0.
- Pre-roll releases at -1.2/-0.85/-0.55/-0.25 s, so frame 0 has candies already bouncing and the first tube entry comes at ~0.2 s. The search rejects any entry before t = 0.
- Characters sit on seat ledges on the outer rims (r 58). Reactions only on lead changes (debounced 0.5 s, 2.2 s gap, none in the last 0.9 s): the new leader laughs, the overtaken one looks worried. Once either tube reaches 80%, both put on a game face and the trailing one sweats.
- Win: in-flight candies shrink-pop silently (14 for s610). The winner hops onto its pile, the tube floor lifts the pile 64 px, then two cheers + confetti + "<NAME> WINS!" at y 572. The loser looks down into its tube, then gives the camera an annoyed look.
- Loop (RESET_S 2.95; was 2.75 — s610's tail read -57.6 dBFS): the floors swing open at 0.45 s and the piles drain in a stream: the column moves at a=3000 px/s², v<=1150, and each candy free-falls once it is past the gate. Counters count down. The frame-0 candies, peg glow and spout puff fade in over the last 0.45 s. Pre-encode diff of the last frame vs frame 0: mean 0.003, max 11/255.
- Sound: entertainer (PD). One walker note per tube landing (+12), peg = quiet pok (0.07 s gate, nudged +12 ms off notes), win arpeggio + pop, cheer triad, latch on the gates, then a descending drain run capped at reset 0.95 s. On s610 the last onset is 2.03 s before the end.
- Search: `--search N [--from K]` keeps a win at 21-29.5 s, a loser of 87-93, >= 3 lead changes, a last change within 5 s of the win that goes to the winner, and a first entry <= 0.35 s. ~6% pass.
