# pressure_reactor — Mochi heats, Bolt holds the lid

- NEW format (2026-10-06, reference idea 5: the popcorn pot whose container fails). A big glass-and-steel reactor fills the frame (0.79 of the width, 0.87 with the springs). It sits on a dark navy-to-plum lab stage. Mochi turns the red gas valve wheel under it. Bolt sits on the domed lid, which also carries a pressure gauge and a steam vent. Caption "Mochi heats, Bolt holds|~Can the lid HOLD 400?~" (y_f 0.098, raised so Bolt clears it at the climax). Counter "Popped n/400" at y 1490.
- Rule: 400 cool crystals (hexagons in sapphire, teal, amethyst and emerald) heat up. Each one glows orange inside, trembles for 0.3 s, then pops: it SWELLS ×1.46 into a magma-style energy orb (ember gradient, white-hot core, dark crust cracks), with a ring, sparks and shell flakes, and it is kicked upward. The pile grows. Kicked orbs knock the lid, then the pile pushes the lid up against two clamp springs, which redden as they stretch. At 400/400 (+0.22 s) the vessel BURSTS.
- Physics: numpy position-based dynamics. 400 circles, all-pairs broad phase once per frame, 4 substeps × 4 Jacobi iterations (relax 0.55), G 2400. The lid is a light 1-D body (mass 1.5 beads, weight accel 2800, spring K 160/px) coupled through the ceiling constraint. Bolt is a 1-D ballistic body riding the lid: the lid's jolts throw him, and he also hops and slams on his own (`stomp_plan`: every 1.3 s → 0.46 s, 400 → 620 px/s). Pop order is a Gumbel draw weighted toward the bottom (scale 380 px). Kick 650-1400 px/s × (0.8 + 0.8 × progress).
- Escalation: pops follow n(t) = 400 × (0.04u + 0.96u^1.8), with u = t/11 s. Pops/s by second: 6, 14, 21, 26, 33, 37, 43, 48, 52, 58, 62. Beats: Mochi's big cranks at 2.6/5.6/8.4 s (wheel spin, flame flare, 3 ratchet ticks) step the flame blue → orange → amber → white-hot. Blue → orange passes through rose, because a straight mix read grey. Steam puffs start at 5.2 s and speed up. Banners: LID LIFTING! at 9.47 s, RED ZONE! at 10.37 s (the gauge crossed red at 10.03), glass cracks spread from about 10 s, BURST! at 11.22 s. Lid rise peaks at about 81 px (t 10-11).
- Burst: the glass breaks into 72 triangle shards that fly with glowing edges. The lid spins off the top, and Bolt is launched (vy -2750, g 3200, 2 flips). The orbs fountain out. A shock ring and a local bloom only, no full-frame flash. Bolt lands sooty and dizzy on the base plate at 13.15 s (with smoke wisps), then stares annoyed at the camera. Mochi laughs at 11.42 s and again at 13.23 s.
- Loop: the reset starts at 14.90 s. The shards and orbs rewind into place while blending to the frame-0 pile, and the orbs shrink and cool back into crystals. The lid drops in from above and Bolt hops back onto it. The last 0.40 s replays the pre-roll (t - T), so the machine state at frame T is frame 0. The wheel angle is re-targeted to the frame-0 trajectory (5 spokes), and the flame flicker and breathing are periodic in T.
