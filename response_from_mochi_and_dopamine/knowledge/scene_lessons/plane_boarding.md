# plane_boarding: BACK-TO-FRONT or RANDOM? which plane is SEATED first? (batch74, NEW)

- Brief: docs/BRIEF_batch74.md (IDEAS.md pick 3). New file `scenes/plane_boarding.py`; no lib changes.
- Fact (measured): Steffen & Hotchkiss, "Experimental test of airplane boarding methods", J. Air Transport Management 18 (2012) 64-67 (arXiv 1108.5211). A mock 757 cabin, 72 passengers, 12 rows of 3-3: 4-row blocks 6:54, back-to-front by row 6:11, random 4:44, WilMA 4:13, Steffen 3:36. Each method was run once (~10 % uncertainty).
- Model (ours): a single-aisle cellular model. One passenger per row of aisle; 0.8 s per row walked; 3-10 s (uniform) to stow a bag; +3 s per seated neighbour who has to get up. The same model and seed (7) for both planes; only the order differs. Mochi = blocks (rows 9-12, 5-8, 1-4; random inside each block); Bolt = random.
- Result (seed 7): random 4:57 (297 s), blocks 6:00 (360 s): ratio 1.21. Over 12 seeds the mean ratio is 1.21 (range 1.07-1.33), random mean 296 s. Measured 2012: 1.46. Our model is MORE conservative than the real test; the direction matches.
- Clip: time-lapse x21; frame 0 = real 20 s (M 2 / B 4 seated). Mochi leads briefly at clip 1.6 s (11 v 10), then Bolt leads from 1.85 s to the end. ALL SEATED at clip 13.20 s, when Mochi has 62/72 seated ("10 still boarding"). Clip 16.0 s (dissolve the last 0.6 s).
- Seated per clip second (both planes): 10 10 11 10 11 10 10 9 9 8 10 8 7 5 (flat, then a slight fall: Bolt's last few, Mochi's front block jammed).
- Drawn: top-down cabins, 54 px row pitch. Passengers are coloured by their row zone (blue rows 1-4, gold 5-8, violet 9-12): blocks read as stripes, random as a mix. A bag floats up to the bin while a passenger stows; blocking neighbours shuffle. The panels show SEATED n/72 + a bar to ALL SEATED + the sim clock.
