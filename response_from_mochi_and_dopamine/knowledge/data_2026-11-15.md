## Headline
1. **The hook gate held up with 30 more Shorts.**
   - Stayed-to-watch vs views: ρ = +0.68 (n=109) on Mochi and +0.83 (n=34) on DopamineEXE.
   - Mochi: 0 of 56 Shorts under 55% stayed reached 5K; 21 of 31 at ≥65% did.
2. **The Mochi hit rate is rising; the median is flat.** Shorts ≥10K by publish week: 0, 0, 3, 3, 4, **8 of 21** (week of Nov 7). The median stays at 2.5-3.8K.
3. **Total channel views are flat:** 37.6K → 42.1K → 42.8K → 43.7K per day over the last 4 weeks. New hits only replace the decaying press_split2 and press_split4.
4. **Nothing is near 300K.** The best Short since Oct 22 is ice_cubes at 40.4K. Only press_split2 (356K) and press_split4 (242K) ever passed 50K.
5. **What a top-decile Short is** (≥18K, n=11):
   - stayed 72%, length 16 s, a FILL meter (64%), a ×N rule (91%), a sequel of a hit (64%), the press family (55%);
   - or an everyday question settled by a spectacle (ice, sandcastle).

7. **Audience affinity is still zero.** Returning viewers: casual 1.9%, regular 0.0%. Subscribers give 0.9% of watch time.
8. **DopamineEXE is not improving.**
   - Median Short 1.5K; 2 of 34 reached ≥10K.
   - 20 of its 34 settled Shorts are its weakest family: "chain until it breaks", stayed 39.5%, median 1.4K.
   - Daily views fell from 6.4K to 3.1K per day.

## 6. Surprises
1. **Day-2 takeoffs keep happening.** jar_versus4 went from 3.6K to 31.3K on day 2, and Crushing the Sun on its day 2. Judging a clip at 24 h undercounts.
2. **Nov 11 sank 3 good clips** (2.1-3.9K, stayed 53-66%), including 2 sequels of hits. It was the weakest channel day since Oct 21.
3. **The 14:45 ET "best slot" is our own scheduling,** not the audience: no effect at equal stayed.
4. **Subscriber churn is 23%** of gains in 4 weeks (112 lost of 496), and almost none of it is attributed to a video.
5. **Bigger hits convert worse:** 0.31 vs 0.66 subs per 1K. Press, the reach engine, converts worst (0.29).
6. **STRANDED, the most-built recent theme, is the weakest data on the channel:** the long video got 248 views and its first Short 24% stayed. 12 STRANDED Shorts are queued.
7. **DopamineEXE spends 59% of its output on its weakest family** (chain until it breaks, 39.5% stayed). Its only repeatable winner is split-to-dust.
8. **Studio's "audience also watches" points to country/flag self-reference and famous-object collisions** (1-1.8M views each), not duels.

## 7. Questions the data cannot answer (for the external researcher and the synthesiser)
1. **What decides whether Browse tests a new long video** from a small channel (168K impressions vs 690)? Title, thumbnail, topic, famous object, upload hour, day-2 retrain? We have n=4.
2. **What is the second gate after stayed%?** 10 Shorts at ≥65% stayed got under 5K. Studio exports no Shorts-feed impressions per video. Is it swipe speed, satisfaction surveys, topic classification, or randomness?
3. **What separates 30K from 300K at equal stayed?** press_tools and press_tools3 (79%) stopped at 25-31K; press_split2 (76%) got 356K. Is it repeated pushes, sharing, comment velocity, or topic breadth? Our shares are too sparse to measure (38 in total).
4. **Do hashtags help distribution?** n=7 leans yes; we need ≥15 matched pairs or a YouTube statement.
5. **Is a day-level effect real** (Nov 11: 3 clips under-delivered)? Needs platform-wide or peer daily data.
6. **Would a fixed series wrapper create returning viewers?** Never tested; returning viewers are ~0.
7. **Does a recognisable character help or hurt?** It can't be separated from the channel here. External evidence on original-character physics channels is needed.
8. **Which DopamineEXE format can graduate?** Scale journey is n=1 at 21K. The country, flag and famous-object formats the Studio audience watches are untested until the Nov 17-21 uploads land.
9. **What RPM?** Neither channel is monetised. The parent's $0.067 (Shorts) vs $1.79 (long) is the only prior. Tier-1 share is 26%.
10. **Made-for-kids exposure:** 13-17 is 19% of signed-in views, and under-13 is never reported. Is there a measurable MFK-classification risk at this share?
11. **YPP thresholds:** Studio still shows 4,000 h / 10M. Does the Feb 2027 change apply to us, and when?
12. **For the synthesiser:** the data argues for FILL+×N, press/jar episodes, everyday-spectacle questions, ≤16 s, sequels inside 14 days, and long-form only when a theme can earn Browse impressions. It argues against the STRANDED Shorts queue and DopamineEXE's chain-until-it-breaks family. The owner decides.
